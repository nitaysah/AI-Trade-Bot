🤖 BotBulls News AI

BotBulls News AI reads the latest articles for the selected ticker, scores the overall tone out of 100 and gives a Bullish, Bearish or Neutral verdict. It does not use charts, indicators or trade levels.

What does it show?

  • Verdict and score: Bullish, Bearish or Neutral, with a 0–100 news score and the classifier's confidence.
  • Summary and main catalyst: A short read of what is driving the news.
  • Articles analysed: Each article's source, age, relevance, sentiment and impact, with the AI's one-line reasoning.
  • Coverage warning: Shown when fewer than 3 relevant articles were found, because the score is weak evidence then.

Manual Refresh & Caching

Cache duration is configurable under ⚙ and defaults to 30 minutes. Click the refresh button (↻) at the top right of the card to bypass the cache and re-read the latest news.

Note: News sentiment is advisory only, is not a probability of profit and does not constitute financial advice.
