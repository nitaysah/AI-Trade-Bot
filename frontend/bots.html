Recommendation

Start with Paper Trading

We strongly recommend all beginners start with a **Paper Account**. This uses $100,000 of "fake money" to simulate real market conditions without risking your capital.

1
Create Alpaca Account

Sign up at Alpaca.markets and verify your email.

2
Choose Environment

In your Alpaca dashboard, toggle to **"Paper Trading"** in the sidebar.

3
Generate API Keys

Look for the **"API Keys"** section and click "Generate New Key". You will get an **API Key ID** and a **Secret Key**.

4
Connect to Bot

Paste those keys into the "Link Account" window here. Make sure the "Paper Trading" toggle matches your Alpaca dashboard setting.

Why Paper First?

  • ✔ master indicator confluence
  • ✔ test different timeframes
  • ✔ build confidence in AI signals

🤖 BotBulls Quant AI Premium Feature

BotBulls Quant AI calculates the final signal, setup score, reward/risk and trade levels with deterministic rules. The LLM explains the result and cannot override the execution gates.

What does it analyze?

  • Technical Confluence: Separately scores trend, momentum, prior-bar structure and volume using the decision timeframe selected under ⚙ settings.
  • Volatility Regimes: Measures ATR, Bollinger width, and current VIX indices to calibrate risk.
  • News & Catalysts: Scores ticker news independently of candle timeframe and uses it only as limited confirmation.
  • Execution Gates: A trade requires the configured setup score, reward/risk, data quality and confirmation rules.

Manual Refresh & Caching

Cache duration is configurable under ⚙ and defaults to 30 minutes. Click the refresh button (↻) at the top right of the card to bypass the cache and fetch a real-time fresh analysis.
