Recommendation

Start with Paper Trading

Start every bot on an Alpaca paper account. It trades with simulated money (Alpaca starts you with $100,000) at real market prices, so you can see exactly how your bot behaves without risking capital.

1
Create Alpaca Account

Sign up for free at alpaca.markets and verify your email.

2
Choose Environment

In your Alpaca dashboard, switch to your Paper Trading account. Paper and live accounts have separate keys.

3
Generate API Keys

In the API Keys panel, click Generate New Keys. Copy the API Key ID and the Secret Key right away; Alpaca shows the secret only once.

4
Connect to Bot

Open Link Account here, paste both keys and keep Paper Trading Enabled switched on. Keys are encrypted before they are saved to your account. Live accounts need an extra confirmation and stay locked until live trading is enabled for your account.

Why Paper First?

  • ✔ see real entries and exits on live prices
  • ✔ compare results with your backtest
  • ✔ learn how stops and the 3-loss auto-pause work

🤖 BotBulls Quant AI Premium Feature

BotBulls Quant AI calculates the final signal, setup score, reward/risk and trade levels with deterministic rules. The LLM explains the result and cannot override the execution gates.

What does it analyze?

  • Technical Confluence: Separately scores trend, momentum, prior-bar structure and volume using the decision timeframe selected under ⚙ settings.
  • Volatility Regimes: Measures ATR, Bollinger width, and current VIX indices to calibrate risk.
  • News & Catalysts: Scores ticker news independently of candle timeframe and uses it only as limited confirmation.
  • Execution Gates: A trade requires the configured setup score, reward/risk, data quality and confirmation rules.

Manual Refresh & Caching

Cache duration is configurable under ⚙ and defaults to 30 minutes. Click the refresh button (↻) at the top right of the card to bypass the cache and fetch a real-time fresh analysis.
