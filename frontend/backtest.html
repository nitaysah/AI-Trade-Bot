Backtesting & Optimization Guide

Master the Alpha Engine

1. Engine Logic & Architecture

The backtesting engine is designed to accurately simulate historical market conditions using your custom algorithmic parameters.

  • Data Ingestion: Fetches historical OHLCV data using Yahoo Finance (yfinance) and optionally Alpaca's historical API. Data is cleaned and filtered for Regular Trading Hours (RTH) if extended hours are disabled.
  • Indicator Engine: Calculates complex technical indicators (RSI, MACD, EMA Cross, Supertrend, Bollinger Bands, VWAP, etc.) across the entire historical timeframe to prevent iterative lag.
  • ATR-Based Stops: Volatility-adjusted Stop Loss & Take Profit using the Average True Range (ATR). Instead of a fixed percentage, the stop loss widens in volatile markets and tightens in quiet markets.

2. Auto-Optimization & Preventing Overfitting

A common trap in algorithmic trading is "Curve Fitting"—tweaking parameters until the backtest looks perfect, only to watch the strategy fail in live markets. To solve this, Bot Bulls includes an Auto-Optimizer using Grid Search and Out-of-Sample (OOS) validation.

  • Grid Search: The engine tests hundreds of combinations of parameters (Signal Thresholds, ATR Multipliers, etc.) simultaneously.
  • Data Splitting: The historical data is split. In-Sample data is used to train and find the best Sharpe Ratio. Out-of-Sample data is unseen "blind" data to test those best parameters.
  • Overfit Badge: If a strategy performs exceptionally well In-Sample but fails Out-of-Sample, it is tagged as 🔴 OVERFITTED. If it maintains its edge, it is tagged as 🟢 ROBUST.

3. How to Get the Best Results (Best Practices)

Avoid Signal Starvation

Selecting 8 different indicators doesn't guarantee a better strategy; it often causes the bot to never trade. Stick to 2-4 complementary indicators.

Prioritize Sharpe Ratio

A strategy with 500% ROI but an 80% Max Drawdown is a gamble. Aim for the highest Sharpe Ratio (>1.5) to ensure consistent growth.

Always Use Optimizer

Never deploy a bot based purely on a standard backtest. Always toggle Auto-Optimizer and rely on the Out-of-Sample validation badge.

Test in Live Trading

Once you find a 🟢 ROBUST strategy, deploy it directly to live trading with Alpaca. Monitor it closely in your active dashboard.
