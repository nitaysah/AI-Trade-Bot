    Backtesting Guide

    Master the Alpha Engine

    1. How a Backtest Works

    A backtest replays your exact bot settings on past candles and shows what those rules would have done. Pick a tab (Regular for stocks & crypto, Options for calls/puts), set the ticker, timeframe, dates and rules, then run it.

    • Price data: Split-adjusted candles (dividends are not added) from Alpaca first, with Yahoo Finance as the fallback. Yahoo only keeps about 7 days of 1-minute and 60 days of 5-minute candles, so link your Alpaca keys for longer intraday tests. Choosing the Regular session keeps only regular-hours candles.
    • Fills: A signal is decided on a completed candle and the trade fills at the next candle's open, the same way a live bot acts. Stop-loss and take-profit levels can trigger inside a candle.
    • No costs added: Fees and slippage are not included, so real results are usually somewhat lower than the backtest.
    • Your rules, as written: The backtest does not apply the live 3-loss auto-pause, and Signal Reset and Smart Re-entry start off here. Turn them on if you use them on your bot. Live position sizing and data-source differences are listed as notes under the results.
    • Strategy presets: Presets such as Golden Cross, Supertrend + 200 EMA, the Malik allocators and Session Rider can be tested with the same rules the bots use. The Malik allocators did not pass our research test, so treat their results with extra caution. Session Rider needs Alpaca intraday data.

    2. Preventing Overfitting

    A common trap in algorithmic trading is "Curve Fitting"—tweaking parameters until the backtest looks perfect, only to watch the strategy fail in live markets. Decide your settings first, then check them on data you did not tune on.

    • Fix settings before testing: Choose the indicators, thresholds and exits from your idea, not from whichever run looked best.
    • Check unseen data: Re-run the same settings on a different period and on other liquid tickers. A strategy that only works on one window is likely curve-fitted.
    • Enough trades: Results from fewer than about 30 trades are mostly noise.
    • Beat Buy & Hold: Compare against the Buy & Hold line in the results, on return and drawdown. If simply holding the stock did as well with less risk, the strategy adds nothing.

    3. How to Get the Best Results (Best Practices)

    Avoid Signal Starvation

    Selecting 8 different indicators doesn't guarantee a better strategy; it often causes the bot to never trade. Stick to 2-4 complementary indicators.

    Judge Risk, Not Just Return

    A 500% return with an 80% max drawdown is a gamble. Read Max Drawdown, Sharpe and Sortino next to the return, and be wary of chasing the single best-looking run.

    Match Your Bot Settings

    Deploy the bot with the same timeframe, session, indicators, thresholds and exits you tested. Any difference means the bot runs a strategy that was not backtested.

    Paper Trade First

    A good backtest is not proof. Run the bot on an Alpaca paper account for several weeks before using real money, and watch it on the Bots page.
